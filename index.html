RESET YOUR MIND

When an urge appears

  1. Put your phone away and leave the triggering situation.
  2. Change rooms or step outside.
  3. Walk for 10 minutes or do a light activity.
  4. Remember why you chose this commitment.
  5. Return to your day without judging yourself.
